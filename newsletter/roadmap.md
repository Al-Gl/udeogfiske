# Newsletter roadmap: November 2026 to April 2027

Six monthly mails. Each one is a draft idea, not finished copy. Themes follow the season, so a mail is always about something people can fish for in the coming weeks.

Written 2026-10-13. Expect it to change: the answers to the welcome mail's question ("Hvad fisker du mest efter?") should steer the later months.

## How each mail is built

Same frame as the October draft in `2026-10/oktober-havorred.html`, so readers learn what to expect:

1. Your own photo and a headline for the month.
2. "Kort fortalt": three or four lines for people who read nothing else.
3. Spot of the month, with an aerial photo and numbered notes.
4. What I do differently this month (three or four points).
5. One piece of gear.
6. Two or three links back to the site.

**Who picks the spot (Aldin, 2026-10-13):** Aldin names the specific spot for each mail. Drafts written before he has named one talk only about the type of water (fjord, inner fjord, open coast, put & take lake) and leave a marked gap for the spot. The type of water listed for each month below is a starting suggestion for him to confirm or change.

## The six mails

### 1. November: sea trout before the closed season

- **Send:** Thursday 5 November 2026
- **Working subject:** "Sidste chance før fredningen"
- **Angle:** Coloured sea trout are protected from 16 November to 15 January. The mail goes out eleven days before, so the hook is: this is what November fishing looks like, and this is what changes on the 16th.
- **Type of water:** fjords. **Spot:** from Aldin. The unsent October draft uses Kyndbyværket with a finished aerial photo; keep it or name another.
- **Content:** what "overspringere" are and why they matter from now on, how to tell a silver fish from a coloured one, what to do if you hook a coloured fish.
- **Gear:** the shallow-water lure from the October draft.
- **Site links:** havørredfiskeri-november, blog/fredning-af-havorred, fiskepladser/isefjorden.
- **Work needed:** small. The October draft was never sent, so most of it can be reused. The headline, intro and the "four things I do differently" section need rewriting for November.

### 2. December: winter fishing and staying warm

- **Send:** Thursday 3 December 2026
- **Working subject:** "Sådan fisker jeg, når det er koldt"
- **Angle:** Fishing does not stop in winter, it slows down. Where the silver fish stand in cold water, which hours of the day are worth it, and how to stay warm enough to keep fishing.
- **Type of water:** inner fjords. **Spot:** from Aldin. Needs a new aerial photo once the spot is named.
- **Content:** slow retrieves and small lures, midday instead of dawn, a short reminder that coloured fish go back.
- **Gear:** neoprene waders, pointing to the buyer's guide. This is the month people buy presents, so add a short "gift ideas for an angler" block: fishing books, a beginner set.
- **Site links:** havørredfiskeri-december, havørredfiskeri-vinter, neopren-waders, fiskeboger, plus the spot page if one exists.
- **Work needed:** medium. New spot photo, new copy.

### 3. January: put & take rainbow trout in the cold

- **Send:** Thursday 7 January 2027
- **Working subject:** "Fisk, der altid er hjemme"
- **Angle:** The coast is at its quietest and sea trout are still protected until the 15th. Put & take lakes are open and stocked all year, which makes January the natural month for them. It also gives the readers who never fish the coast a mail of their own.
- **Type of water:** put & take lake. **Spot:** from Aldin. If he names a lake he has a trip report from, his own photos can replace the aerial photo.
- **Content:** where the fish stand in a cold lake, how deep to fish, and what Aldin changes when the first method does not work. One closing line: the sea trout closed season ends on 15 January, more on that next month.
- **Gear:** a spinner for cold water.
- **Site links:** regnbueorredfiskeri, spinnefiskeri-i-put-take, plus the lake page if one exists.
- **Work needed:** medium. New copy; photo depends on the lake.

### 4. February: sea trout is open again

- **Send:** Thursday 4 February 2027
- **Working subject:** "Milde dage og lavt vand"
- **Angle:** The closed season is over and the first mild days bring fish onto shallow water. February rewards picking the right day more than any other month.
- **Type of water:** fjords and sheltered shallow water. **Spot:** from Aldin. Needs a new aerial photo once the spot is named.
- **Content:** how to read the forecast for a February trip, shallow dark bottom that warms first, slow fishing.
- **Gear:** wading jacket, pointing to the jacket chooser.
- **Site links:** havørredfiskeri-februar, vadejakke, plus the spot page if one exists.
- **Work needed:** medium. New spot photo, new copy.

### 5. March: spring starts on the coast

- **Send:** Thursday 4 March 2027
- **Working subject:** "Nu vågner kysten"
- **Angle:** March is the first real spring month for sea trout. People who put the rod away in November are coming back, so this is the "get ready" mail.
- **Type of water:** fjord mouths and the open coast. **Spot:** from Aldin. Needs a new aerial photo once the spot is named.
- **Content:** what changes from winter to spring fishing, a short check of your gear before the season (line, hooks, waders).
- **Gear:** spring through-line lures. That page already gets clicks from Google, and March is when people buy lures, so this is the strongest gear month of the six.
- **Site links:** havørredfiskeri-marts, bedste-gennemlobsblink-havorred-forar, plus the spot page if one exists.

### 6. April: the best month

- **Send:** Thursday 1 April 2027
- **Working subject:** "April: tre fisk på én måned"
- **Angle:** Sea trout at its spring peak, spring herring close to land from late March to mid-May, and garfish on the way in. The widest mail of the six, and the one most likely to bring beginners out.
- **Type of water:** open coast for sea trout, harbour or pier for herring. **Spot:** from Aldin.
- **Content:** herring from a pier with a simple rig, sea trout in April in two or three lines, a short "garfish arrive in May" teaser for the next mail.
- **Gear:** herring rig. If the welcome-mail answers show many beginners, swap it for the beginner set.
- **Site links:** sildefiskeri, havørredfiskeri-april, hornfisk, plus the spot page if one exists.

## Why these themes

- **Sea trout carries four of six mails.** It is what the site is strongest on, it has a page for every month, and winter and spring are its season.
- **No cod.** It is prohibited to catch in most of Denmark, so a cod mail would send people towards fishing they mostly can't do.
- **No mackerel or garfish before April.** Both are out of season all winter. They belong in the May to August mails.
- **No region comes first.** Readers come from all of Denmark, so the advice in each mail is written by type of water and should work on any coast. Aldin picks the spots and decides how they are spread across the country.
- **January goes to put & take** because it is fishable in any weather and reaches a different reader than the coast mails.
